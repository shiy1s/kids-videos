# HAPPY HIPPO Character Bible — Canon v1

This document locks the current original character identities. Do not redraw, recolor, change proportions, clothes, accessories, or voice IDs without an explicit owner-approved version change.

## Bibi — lead friend
- Role/age: preschool bunny friend, approximately 4.
- Personality: curious, affectionate, enthusiastic, sometimes needs a moment to think.
- Silhouette: rounded mint/soft-green bunny; long rounded ears; large expressive eyes; small pink inner ears and cheeks.
- Outfit/signature: teal overalls with a round yellow button.
- Voice direction: warm, bright, gentle, medium-high pitch, clear consonants, unhurried. Fixed voice ID: NOT YET VERIFIED.
- Movement signature: two small happy bounces, ear sway, blink, open-arm greeting.
- Emotions required: happy, curious, sad-but-safe, surprised, reassuring.

## Tiko — steady friend
- Role/age: preschool turtle friend, approximately 4.
- Personality: patient, thoughtful, dependable, playful.
- Silhouette: rounded peach/soft-orange face, small round body, large eyes.
- Outfit/signature: leafy green shell/backpack, sunny orange cap.
- Voice direction: gentle, friendly, slightly lower than Bibi but still child-safe; slow and clearly articulated. Fixed voice ID: NOT YET VERIFIED.
- Movement signature: short waddling steps, shell/body bounce, small wave.
- Emotions required: happy, curious, disappointed-but-safe, surprised, reassuring.

## Momo — bird sidekick
- Role/age: tiny bird companion, childlike, no stated human age.
- Personality: observant, cheerful, encouraging.
- Silhouette: peach bird, small golden beak, round eyes.
- Outfit/signature: lilac cap with a yellow dot.
- Voice direction: light, soft, playful, clearly distinguishable from Bibi and Tiko. Fixed voice ID: NOT YET VERIFIED.
- Movement signature: wing flutters, tiny hops, head tilts.

## Locations and palette locks
1. Garden/park: mint grass, warm cream sky, leafy greens, small coral/yellow flowers.
2. Home/playroom: cream walls, warm wood, sky-blue windows, uncluttered props.
3. Pond/beach: sky blue, aqua water, cream highlights, safe open space.
4. Bedtime room/night garden: muted lavender/navy, soft moonlight, no frightening shadows.

## Art direction
Original rounded 2D puppet style, large expressive eyes, consistent line weight, soft shadows, gentle saturated colors, layered foreground/midground/background. No resemblance target to any existing studio or copyrighted character.

## Required reusable transparent PNG layers
For each character: body, head, left/right arms, left/right legs, eyes open/closed, happy/sad/surprised eyebrows, mouth closed/A/E/O/U/M-B-P/F-V/smile/sad. Add a neutral mouth and phoneme timing map for lip-sync. Use the same source layers across every episode.
