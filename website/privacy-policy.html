Privacy Policy — Shiyas Kids Videos

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

Shiyas Kids Videos is a personal automation project that creates original, child-friendly cartoon videos and uploads them to a YouTube channel managed by the project owner. This website does not offer user accounts or collect information directly from children.

2. Google and YouTube data

The project uses Google OAuth and YouTube APIs to authorize and perform video-publishing actions on the project owner's channel. Depending on the operation, these services may return account or video metadata needed to upload a video and verify the result.

3. How data is used

Information accessed through Google OAuth is used to authorize uploads, submit video files and metadata, and confirm publishing results. It is not sold and is not used to build advertising profiles. The project does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its videos.

4. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored as private GitHub Actions repository secrets rather than in source code. Video files and temporary processing artifacts may be handled by GitHub Actions during generation, rendering, quality checks, and publishing. Access to the repository and its secrets should be limited to authorized maintainers.

5. Retention and deletion

Temporary build artifacts are retained only as needed for processing or debugging and according to the hosting platform's configured retention. Uploaded videos remain on YouTube until the channel owner changes their settings or removes them. The project owner can revoke the application's Google access from their Google Account security settings.

6. Third-party services

Google and YouTube handle information under their own policies and terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the project or its data practices change. The updated date above will be revised when material changes are made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project owner using the contact information displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
