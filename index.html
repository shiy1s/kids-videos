Shiyas Kids Videos

Shiyas Kids Videos is a personal project for creating original, child-friendly cartoon stories with procedural artwork, locally generated narration, and video-rendering tools.

The project uses YouTube's authorized services to publish and manage videos on the project owner's channel. It does not provide a public sign-in service or ask children to submit personal information.

Questions about privacy or the project can be directed to the project owner through the contact information shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
