# Privacy Policy — Kids YouTube Automation

Last updated: October 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

Kids YouTube Automation is a personal automation project that generates
original, child-friendly cartoon videos and uploads them to a YouTube
channel managed by the project owner.

## 2. Information and Permissions

The application uses Google OAuth to authorize uploads to the project
owner's YouTube channel.

Depending on the operation, Google may provide account and video
information necessary to perform and verify uploads.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information
from children through its videos.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google OAuth is used to:
- Authorize video uploads.
- Upload video titles, descriptions, and video files.
- Confirm the result of publishing operations.

Information is not sold to third parties.

## 4. Credential Security

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens used by the automation are stored
as GitHub Actions repository secrets rather than in the source code.

Access to these credentials should be restricted to authorized users.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The project uses Google and YouTube services for authentication and
video publishing. Their handling of information is governed by their
respective privacy policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

YouTube Terms of Service:
https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

## 6. Data Retention

Temporary video-generation artifacts may be retained by the automation
platform for a limited period to support debugging.

YouTube retains uploaded videos according to the selected publishing
settings and the channel owner's actions.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project changes.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions about this project, contact the project owner
through the contact information provided on the application's OAuth
consent screen.
